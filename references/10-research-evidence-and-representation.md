# Research, Claims, Evidence & Representation

## Claim States
- `verified`
- `unverified`
- `disputed`
- `inference`

High-risk exact claims should not enter narration/evidence graphics while unverified.

## Claim Specificity Invariant
A downstream representation must not become more specific than its supporting claim.

Examples:
- `billions in mobile R&D` cannot become `$5B touchscreen R&D`
- `weaker app ecosystem` cannot become `NO APPS`
- a quarterly statistic cannot silently become an annual statistic

Stylistic compression may shorten language but may not change truth conditions.

## Representation Types
### literal
Direct depiction of a real identifiable thing/event.

### evidence
Faithful statistic/chart/document/quote/screenshot presented as factual support. Must map to verified claims/sources.

### reconstruction
Approximate reenactment/representation based on evidence. Must not masquerade as an original primary source.

### metaphor
Intentional symbolic physicalization.

### abstract
Diagram/system model with no claim of historical appearance.

## Fake Evidence Prohibition
Never fabricate:
- archival memo/audit
- newspaper headline presented as real
- screenshot
- quote
- evidence stamp
- exact statistic

If an invented document-like prop is used as metaphor, keep its visible wording generic and non-evidentiary.

## Rights != Accuracy
Track factual accuracy and usage rights independently.
