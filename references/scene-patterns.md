# Scene Patterns

Use these as concept-search families, not templates. Select the family that best makes the relationship visible.

## Transformation and state change

The same object or environment changes category, condition, role, or meaning. Useful for before/after, deterioration, redesign, replacement, and recontextualization.

## Cause and effect

A visible trigger produces a visible consequence. Keep the connection on screen; do not rely on a cut between unrelated symbols to imply causality.

## Process and handoff

Materials, information, money, or responsibility move through connected stages. Prefer one continuing object, route, or camera path when continuity is the point.

## Flow, route, and geography

Movement across a map, system, supply chain, timeline, or physical space reveals scale and dependency.

## Accumulation, depletion, and burden

Objects, capacity, users, obligations, energy, or resources visibly build or drain. Use when the quantity's direction matters more than an exact chart.

## Constraint and bottleneck

Flow narrows, queues, jams, fails to connect, or competes for limited capacity.

## Network and feedback loop

Nodes and flows reinforce or weaken one another. Make the loop's causal return visible rather than drawing decorative orbiting icons.

## Behavioral comparison

Two systems perform the same job differently. Show behavior or consequence instead of a static two-column list when possible.

## Evidence inspection

A real chart, document, screenshot, map, or object becomes the environment. Preserve context while highlighting only what the source supports.

## Object-as-data

Physical or reconstructed objects embody a quantity. Exact geometry must match verified data; illustrative scale must remain clearly illustrative.

## Character and gesture

A portrait, hand, or prop action communicates a social or emotional turn. Use only when the gesture carries meaning beyond decoration.

## Reveal and recontextualization

The camera or layers expose that an established object/world means something different than assumed.

## Continuous stage

One coherent world contains several beats; camera travel and object continuity connect them. This is often stronger than repeated cards for processes, but it is not required for unrelated evidence beats.

## Conflict is optional

Use actor/counter-force/stakes only when real conflict exists. Never invent a villain to make motion dramatic.

## Selection test

Choose the family that:

- makes the thesis readable muted;
- uses story-specific materials;
- creates a meaningful state change;
- remains factually honest;
- differs usefully from adjacent scenes;
- can be built with accepted assets and available time.
