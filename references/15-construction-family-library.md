# Construction Family Library

These are starting geometries for concept search, not four “universal video types.” A film may combine several.

## Editorial Narrative Stage
Characters/props/documents inhabit a tactile environment and physically perform story events.
Best for: business/history/character stories.

## Data Evidence
Charts, numbers, maps and source evidence animate as proof rather than decoration.
Best for: quantified claims, comparisons, economic/scientific evidence.

## Continuous Kinetic Timeline
One large world/stage contains milestones; camera moves between them.
Best for: product evolution, historical sequences, process over time.

## UI / Product Demonstration
Interface layers, cursors, states and transformations show product behavior.
Best for: software demos, feature explainers, workflows.

## Mechanism / Process Explainer
Parts, flows and transformations expose how a system works.
Best for: technical/scientific/business mechanisms.

## Map / Geography
Routes, regions, chokepoints, movement and scale live on a geographic stage.
Best for: geopolitics, logistics, travel, supply chains.

## Network / Ecosystem
Nodes, loops, flows and reinforcement make system momentum visible.
Best for: platforms, social systems, developer ecosystems, finance.

## Evidence / Document Investigation
Real documents/screenshots/charts become the environment; camera inspects and annotates.
Best for: investigations, quotes, archival proof.

## Transformation / Before-After
Same object/environment changes state in place.
Best for: category shifts, redesigns, deterioration, improvement.

## Object-as-Chart
Physical/reconstructed objects embody quantities.
Best for: memorable statistics when literal bars feel sterile.

## Character / Gesture
Portrait/cutout/hand/prop action communicates a social/emotional beat.
Best for: rejection, warning, negotiation, reaction.

## Accumulation / Burden
Objects pile/build/multiply until scale or pressure becomes obvious.
Best for: debt, volume, complexity, growth, backlog.

## Constraint / Bottleneck
Flow narrows, blocks or becomes incompatible.
Best for: throughput, logistics, fragmented software, policy constraints.

## Recontextualization / Reveal
Camera or layer change reveals that the object/world means something different than assumed.
Best for: twists, final reframes, hidden mechanisms.

## Selection
Generate concepts across different families when uncertainty is high. Choose the family that makes the beat clearest muted and differs usefully from adjacent scenes.
