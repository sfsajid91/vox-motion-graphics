# Autopilot & Brief Normalization

## Goal
Allow a user with no motion-design vocabulary to provide a vague brief while the system makes professional directing decisions.

## Minimal Input
A usable request can be as little as:
`Make an engaging short about why container shipping changed global trade.`

Optional inputs:
- platform / aspect ratio
- duration
- audience
- brand/style reference
- supplied research/script/assets
- budget or tool constraints

Do not require them.

## Director Brief
Infer and record:
- `viewerPromise`: what the viewer should understand/feel by the end
- `format`: platform, aspect ratio, duration envelope
- `audience`: expertise and context
- `tone`: analytical, playful, ominous, intimate, etc.
- `factuality`: factual / illustrative / fictional / mixed
- `emotionalArc`: e.g. curiosity -> tension -> recognition -> payoff
- `materialWorld`: physical nouns/materials naturally associated with the subject
- `contrastPair`: the story's strongest visual opposition when one exists
- `productionConstraints`: no GPU, rights requirements, brand rules, etc.

## Material-World Rule
Style and metaphor should grow from the subject.

Examples:
- shipping -> steel, containers, cranes, routes, ports, stamps
- semiconductors -> wafers, cleanrooms, dies, traces, microscopes
- banking -> ledgers, signatures, counters, vault mechanisms
- software -> windows, nodes, paths, cursors, stacks, terminals

Do not arbitrarily apply film grain or paper collage when the subject suggests a stronger visual world.

## Contrast Pair
Useful contrasts can organize an entire film:
- physical vs digital
- old vs new
- centralized vs distributed
- abundance vs scarcity
- speed vs bottleneck
- surface appearance vs hidden mechanism
- leader vs challenger

A contrast pair is optional. Do not invent an enemy when the story has none.

## Autopilot Decisions
The system may decide without asking:
- scene count
- scene grouping
- visual metaphors
- camera grammar
- art-directed text
- style profile
- asset source strategy
- SFX vocabulary
- transitions
- motion cadence

Ask only when:
- facts are missing and cannot be safely generalized;
- rights/permissions require a real user decision;
- a brand/legal constraint is unknowable;
- two options materially change editorial intent or cost.
